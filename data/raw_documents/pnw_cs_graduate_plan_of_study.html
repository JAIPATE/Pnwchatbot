Master of Science in Computer Science: Electronic Plan of Study (EPOS)

Official Degree Roadmap, Core Course Requirements, and Graduate School Submission Procedures

Electronic Plan of Study (EPOS) Overview

Every graduate student admitted to the Master of Science in Computer Science (MS CS) program at Purdue University Northwest must draft and submit an official Electronic Plan of Study (EPOS). The EPOS acts as an academic contract between the graduate student, the Department of Computer Science, and the Purdue Graduate School.

Submission Deadline: Graduate students must formally submit an approved EPOS prior to completing fifteen (15) graduate credit hours (typically before the end of their second semester of enrollment). Failure to submit an approved EPOS by this milestone results in a registration hold for subsequent terms.

Step-by-Step EPOS Submission Workflow

  1. Access myPNW: Log in to the official myPNW student portal at https://mypnw.pnw.edu using your Purdue Career Account credentials.
  2. Navigate to Graduate Services: Click on the Academic Services tab and select the Graduate School Plan of Study (EPOS) link.
  3. Initiate Draft Plan: Create a new Plan of Study and designate "Computer Science - MS" as your official degree objective.
  4. Designate Committee Chair: Enter the name and Purdue ID of your primary Graduate Academic Advisor as Advisory Committee Chair. For non-thesis options, two additional faculty committee members are typically assigned by the department.
  5. Populate Course Credits: Enter 30 graduate credit hours (minimum requirement), ensuring core algorithm, architecture, and systems requirements are fulfilled.
  6. Submit for Advisor Clearance: Submit the completed draft through the electronic portal for primary advisor review.

Mandatory Advisor Approval & Course Substitutions

Submitting an EPOS via myPNW does NOT automatically activate or approve the degree plan. The submitted plan must undergo systematic sequential approval by:

  1. The Advisory Committee Chair (Graduate Advisor).
  2. All appointed Advisory Committee Faculty Members.
  3. The Computer Science Department Head or Graduate Program Director.
  4. The Dean of the Purdue Graduate School.

Course Changes: Any deviation from courses listed on an approved EPOS requires a formal Electronic Plan of Study Amendment. Students must never assume course substitutions are valid without prior written approval from their academic advisor.

Doctoral (Ph.D.) Studies Clarification

Purdue University Northwest confers Master of Science degrees in Computer Science, Mechanical Engineering, and Electrical Engineering. PNW does not offer an independent on-campus Ph.D. program in Computer Science. Students aspiring to earn a doctorate in Computer Science must apply directly to the doctoral program at Purdue University West Lafayette, or participate in designated collaborative research fellowships under joint advisory committees.
