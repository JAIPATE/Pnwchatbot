Purdue University Northwest Academic Regulations & Enrollment Guidelines

Official Policies on Registration PINs, Course Add/Drop Deadlines, and Student Holds

Mandatory Registration PIN Requirement

At Purdue University Northwest (PNW), all degree-seeking undergraduate and graduate students must obtain a unique, six-digit Registration PIN before registering for courses in myPNW each fall, spring, and summer semester.

Registration PINs are NOT released automatically and CANNOT be issued by the Office of the Registrar, academic department front desks, or IT support helpdesks. To receive your Registration PIN, you MUST schedule an advising appointment with your assigned Academic Advisor. During the advising meeting, your advisor will review your degree audit, verify required course prerequisites, and formally release your semester PIN.

Semester Add/Drop Deadlines & Tuition Refund Schedule

Course schedule adjustments are subject to strict academic calendar deadlines:

  • Week 1 (100% Refund Period): Students may add open course sections or drop classes through myPNW without academic penalty or record on official transcripts. 100% tuition refund applies.
  • Weeks 2 through 4 (Partial Refund Period): Dropped courses receive an 80% or 50% tuition refund depending on the specific date published in the PNW Academic Schedule. No refund is granted after the fourth week of the semester.
  • Weeks 2 through 9 (Withdrawal Period): Courses dropped during this window are recorded on the student's official academic transcript with a grade of 'W' (Withdrawal). A grade of 'W' does not affect the student's cumulative grade point average (GPA) but counts towards attempted credit hours for Financial Aid Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP).
  • After Week 9: Course drops are strictly prohibited unless approved through a formal Extenuating Circumstance Petition signed by the Academic Department Head, College Dean, and the Dean of Students.

Student Account Holds & Resolution

Student account holds restrict various institutional services, including registration, grade view access, and transcript issuance. Holds are placed by individual university administrative offices:

  • Bursar Hold: Caused by unpaid tuition, housing fees, or lab charges. Must be resolved directly with the Bursar / Student Financial Services Office in Lawshe Hall Room 130 (Hammond) or LSF Room 101 (Westville).
  • Parking Hold: Triggered by overdue parking citations older than 14 days. Must be paid via the online Parking Portal or at the Bursar office.
  • Immunization / Medical Hold: Placed by Student Health Center due to incomplete vaccination records required by Indiana state law.
  • Dean of Students Disciplinary Hold: Placed in connection with pending student code of conduct or academic integrity investigations.

Automated chatbots and academic advisors do not have administrative permissions to remove or override institutional holds. Students must contact the specific office that placed the restriction.
