Purdue University Northwest: Office of the Dean of Students

Campus Locations, Student Advocacy, Emergency Hardship Assistance, and Grievance Support

Mission & Advocacy Services

The PNW Office of the Dean of Students serves as the primary advocate for undergraduate and graduate students across both campuses. The Dean of Students assists with navigating university policies, coordinating emergency financial assistance grants, addressing mental health crisis referrals, mediating student-faculty grievances, and overseeing the Student Code of Conduct.

Emergency Financial Assistance & Food Pantry

Students facing unforeseen financial hardship, housing instability, or food insecurity may request support through the Dean of Students Emergency Assistance Fund. Additionally, PNW operates campus food pantries at the Hammond campus (SUL 313) and Westville campus (LSF 103), accessible to all currently enrolled students with a valid PNW student ID.

Academic Integrity & Grade Appeal Petitions

Students seeking guidance on the official university grade appeal procedure or who are involved in an academic integrity inquiry can request a confidential consultation with a Dean of Students Student Advocate. The advocate helps students understand procedural rights, prepare appeal documentation, and navigate meetings with academic department heads.
