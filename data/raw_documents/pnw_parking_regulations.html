Purdue University Northwest Traffic & Parking Regulations

Official Parking Rules, Citation Payment, and Virtual Permit Enforcement

Virtual Parking Permit System

Purdue University Northwest (PNW) utilizes virtual permit enforcement tied directly to vehicle license plates at both the Hammond and Westville campuses. Physical hangtags or window decals are no longer issued. All enrolled students, faculty, and university staff must register their active vehicle license plate through the official PNW Parking Portal before parking in university-designated lots.

Students may register up to two vehicles under a single permit; however, only one registered vehicle is permitted on campus property at any given time. Temporary loaner vehicles or rental cars must be registered immediately prior to parking on campus.

Paying Parking Citations & Deadlines

Parking citations issued at the PNW Hammond or Westville campus must be paid or officially appealed within fourteen (14) calendar days of citation issuance.

Payment can be submitted electronically through the Student Accounts Portal accessible within myPNW, or in person at the Bursar / Student Accounts Office on either campus (Lawshe Hall Room 130 in Hammond, or the Student-Faculty Building Room 101 in Westville). Accepted payment methods include credit/debit card, electronic check (ACH), or cashier check.

Failure to settle unpaid parking citations within 14 calendar days will trigger an automated Registrar and Transcripts hold on the student's myPNW academic account. This hold prevents future course registration, add/drop changes, diploma issuance, and official transcript release until all outstanding violation balances are resolved.

Citation Appeals Procedure

Students who believe a parking citation was issued in error or due to verifiable extenuating circumstances may file an appeal. All appeals must be filed electronically through the official PNW Parking Citation Appeal Form on the Parking Services portal within 14 calendar days from the date of the violation. Verbal appeals or phone calls are not accepted.

The PNW Parking Appeals Committee reviews submitted documentation, time-stamped license plate photographs, and portal logs. Decisions rendered by the Appeals Committee are final and communicated via official student email (@pnw.edu).

Campus Parking Zones (Hammond vs. Westville)

Parking regulations vary slightly between campuses:

  • Hammond Campus: Enforces designated student parking lots (Lots 1, 2, and 3). Parking in faculty/staff restricted spaces, designated visitor stalls, or marked maintenance bays without authorization is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate ticketing and immobilization.
  • Westville Campus: Student permits are valid across all designated general campus student parking areas. Restricted spaces include ADA accessible stalls (requiring state placards) and university service vehicles.

Important Note on Purdue West Lafayette Parking

Purdue University Northwest parking permits and parking rules are entirely independent from Purdue University West Lafayette. West Lafayette parking garage permits, 'C' permits, and residence hall parking passes are NOT valid at PNW Hammond or Westville campuses. Conversely, a PNW parking permit confers no authorization to park in any lot or garage at Purdue West Lafayette.
