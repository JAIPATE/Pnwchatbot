# Stakeholder Interview Notes

## Interviewee 1: Director, PNW Dean of Students Office
- **Summary**: Students struggle to find answers to common university questions because information is spread across multiple web pages and policy documents.
- **Impact**: University staff spend a significant amount of time answering repetitive questions.
- **Goal**: Implement a knowledge-based chatbot that students can use to retrieve quick, accurate answers grounded in official documentation.

## Interviewee 2: Jane (Student Service Coordinator)
- **Common Topic Areas**:
  - Adding or dropping classes
  - Academic standing & probation policies
  - Grade appeals process
  - Financial aid deadlines
  - Registration procedures & PINs
  - Directing students to the appropriate department / contact person
- **Critical Requirement / Concern**: The chatbot MUST NOT provide incorrect, hallucinated, or unsupported policy information. Answers must be 100% grounded.

## Target User Definition
- **In-Scope Users**: Currently registered PNW Undergraduate and Graduate students.
- **Out-of-Scope Users**: Faculty, staff, and prospective students (for initial release scope).
