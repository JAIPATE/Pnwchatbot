# User Interview Notes

## Student Interview 1
- **Questions Asked**:
  1. How do you pay parking tickets?
  2. Does PNW offer a Computer Science PhD program?
  3. How to fix a class registration error?
- **Search Process & Experience**:
  - Tried Google search, My PNW portal, then PNW rules/department pages.
  - Search results returned links to general pages, but failed to answer direct questions.
  - Ended up having to ask academic advisor or wait for email responses.
- **Frustrations & Pain Points**:
  - Multiple campuses (Hammond & Westville) make finding campus-specific info difficult.
  - The existing chatbot ("Leo") only provides generic links instead of actual answers.
  - Personalized registration issues and error messages are hard to troubleshoot online.

## Student Interview 2 (Graduate Student)
- **Questions Asked**:
  1. How to submit the Master's in Computer Science Plan of Study (POS), required courses, advisor approvals, and submission deadlines?
- **Search Process & Experience**:
  - Searched PNW website and student portal.
  - Found partial info, but formatting was confusing and spread across different department pages.
  - Had to check latest emails/announcements and consult academic advisor for verification.
- **Frustrations & Pain Points**:
  - Navigating different colleges (e.g., College of Engineering and Science vs. College of Technology) is confusing.
  - Outdated or inconsistent information exists across different pages.
- **Desired Solutions**:
  - A single centralized platform giving clear, simple explanations with official source links.
  - A chatbot that fails safely and directs students to proper departments when uncertain.

## Student Interview 3
- **Questions Asked**:
  1. Graduation application process, course prerequisites, registration PINs.
- **Search Process & Experience**:
  - Information hidden deep under multiple nested links; students had to bookmark multiple browser tabs.
  - Tasks in "MyPNW" portal do not always update status correctly.
  - Course prerequisites require searching multiple levels deep.
- **Frustrations & Pain Points**:
  - Inconsistent or outdated information leading to miscommunication (e.g., students missing required graduation courses and taking extra semesters).
  - Frequent advisor rotation makes relying solely on advisors risky.
  - Abrupt changes (e.g., classroom relocations) are hard to track.

## Summary of Key Pain Points Across All Interviews
1. **Scattered Information**: Info is spread across portal, main site, college sub-sites, and PDFs.
2. **Inconsistency & Outdated Info**: Hard to identify which rule/policy is active and accurate.
3. **Ineffective Existing Solutions**: Current chatbot ("Leo") only links without answering; site search yields irrelevant results.
4. **Prerequisite & Academic Path Ambiguity**: Complex course dependencies are hard to map manually.
5. **Slow Resolution**: Students forced to wait for advisor emails for simple policy questions.
